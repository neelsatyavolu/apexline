One window · race data

Built for dense race watching.

Apexline puts video, timing, schedule, standings, news, and analysis tools in one dark macOS workspace.

Live Timing

A 22-car timing tower with gaps, intervals, sector splits, mini-sector tones, tyre compounds, and tyre age.

Multi-view onboards

Arrange the world feed and selected onboard cameras in a resizable window, with saved layouts for race and replay viewing.

AI race insights

When AI is configured, Apexline can summarize race context, battle notes, strategy windows, and weekend previews from the app's loaded data.

Schedule & standings

Session times, weekend status, countdowns, driver standings, and constructor standings in the same app.

News & analytics

F1 news, saved stories, and post-session comparisons for pace deltas, stints, tyres, and driver head-to-heads.

Native & fast

An Electron macOS app with a packaged renderer, system notifications, and window layouts that adapt to common laptop and desktop sizes.
